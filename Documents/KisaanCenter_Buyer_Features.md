# KisaanCenter Buyer Features & Capabilities

## What Buyer Has
- Access to available products, prices, and shop details
- Dashboard for purchase history and payment status

## What Buyer Can Do
- Browse and purchase flowers/products from shop(s)
- Pay immediately or request credit (if allowed)
- View and download digital receipts
- Track outstanding payments and due dates
- Return or exchange products (as permitted)
- View audit logs for their transactions
- Resolve disputes with shop using system records

---

Buyer is responsible for purchasing products and managing payments in KisaanCenter.
