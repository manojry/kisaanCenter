# KisaanCenter Farmer Features & Capabilities

## What Farmer Has
- Access to their deliveries, stock, sales, and payment status
- Dashboard for tracking products and payments

## What Farmer Can Do
- Deliver flowers/products to shop(s)
- View stock status and sales for each delivery
- Request payments and track pending/completed payments
- View commission deductions and payment breakdowns
- Add comments or notes to deliveries (as permitted)
- View audit logs for their transactions
- Resolve disputes with owner/shop using system records

---

Farmer is responsible for supplying products and tracking their sales and payments in KisaanCenter.
